# CatalogVector — Gate A Outreach

**Date:** 4 August 2026 (revised 6 August 2026, DIRECTIVE-19 §5.1)
**Target:** 25 contacts total — merchants and brands (inverted split — the brand hypothesis is the one that needs the sample). Send 5 now, reach 25 within three weeks as the study measures more stores.
**Gate:** one paid pilot at ≥€2,500 within 8–10 weeks of first outbound contact. The €500 tier is removed.

---

## Before you send anything

**Every email must carry a finding about the recipient's own catalogue.** A cold email with a general statistic is a newsletter; a cold email containing a checkable fact about their SKUs is a favour. That difference is most of your reply rate.

- **Merchants:** you have measurements for Two Step Performance, MAPerformance, Subimods, Springrates, Intec Racing, JDMuscle, Unity Performance, Brembo Store, EBC Brake Shop, Valvetronic. Send to stores you have already run.
- **Brands:** you do **not** yet have per-brand attribution measured. That is DIRECTIVE-18 §3 and it is a few hours of work. **Run it before sending B1–B3.** For each brand, pick 10–15 of their parts and record, per part, which sellers carry it in the Catalog. A brand email without a brand-specific finding is just a pitch.

**Language discipline, in every message:**

- "not detected in the catalogue surface we measured" — never "invisible," never "absent" unqualified
- state the Shopify-only limitation once, briefly. In cold outreach a disclosed limitation reads as competence, not weakness
- no causal claim, no fix claim, no "AI visibility" framing
- mention that there is a paid piece. Otherwise you collect interesting-research replies and learn nothing about demand

## Sending mechanics

Plain text, from your own domain, no HTML, no tracking pixels, no attachments — attachments and tracking are the two fastest routes to spam filtering on cold mail. One link maximum, to the public repo or methodology page.

Send Tuesday to Thursday, morning in the recipient's timezone. **One follow-up only**, seven days later, three lines: *"Following up on the below — happy to send the [store/brand] list either way, no call needed. If it's not relevant, just say so and I'll stop."* That last clause raises reply rates and gives you a clean negative.

Send the first 5 within 48 hours. **The clock starts at the first one.** Reach 25 within three weeks as the study measures more stores.

---

## On the call — the three questions, and nothing else

1. Have you ever checked whether ChatGPT or Copilot surfaces your products? **How would you check?**
2. Has anyone above you asked about it?
3. If some of your SKUs weren't in that catalogue — who would that go to, and would it be a budget line?

**Question 1 does double duty.** If merchants answer "it's in my admin under sales channels," you've learned the audit product has no floor *and* that they don't value it. That's the DIRECTIVE-18 §2 test, answered for free.

**Do not pitch.** Answer what they ask, send the free list, and let them raise the paid version. If they don't raise it, the answer to Gate A is no.

**Never show one recipient another's data.** Named per-store results stay unpublished — that's PUB-1 and it's also the reason they'll trust you with theirs.

## The read — pre-registered, not adjustable

**Applies at n=25 contacts, not n=5.** At n=5, a generous 20% reply rate gives P(≥3 substantive replies) ≈ 5.8% — the pre-registered read is unreachable. At n=25, the same rate gives P(≥3) ≈ 76%. Send 5 now, reach 25 within three weeks. The read applies at 25.

- **≤1 substantive reply out of 25** → the framing is wrong. Recraft and resend once. **Not a demand finding.**
- **≥3 substantive replies, none naming an internal owner or a budget line** → **this is the demand finding.** No buyer exists at present.
- **Anyone paying €2,500, or asking about recurring before you mention it** → Gate A is live.

A sympathetic contact paying out of curiosity is not a signal. A stranger paying is. Someone asking "how often would you re-run this?" before you bring it up is the strongest signal available and it's worth more than the money.

**Window: 8–10 weeks from first contact.** If unmet, stop. That was recorded in advance as a successful outcome and it still is.
